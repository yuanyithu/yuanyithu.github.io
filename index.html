Research

Research Focus

Quantum error mitigation

Developing probabilistic error cancellation protocols that combine post-selection and quantum error-detecting codes to reduce sampling overhead on noisy devices.

Classical shadows

Using tensor-network methods to analyze the error-mitigation complexity of classical shadows in noisy logarithmic-depth quantum circuits.

Quantum annealing

Studying complexity mechanisms in quantum annealing and classical QMC through imaginary-time spacetime instanton action.

Yi Yuan, Yuanchen Zhao, and Dong E. Liu
