Interest

My current focus is quantum error correction and the implementation-level problems that stand between QEC theory and working fault-tolerant devices.

My previous work develops error-mitigation techniques for the noisy and early fault-tolerant regime — combining quantum error-detecting codes with probabilistic error cancellation to reshape the effective noise channel and suppress residual logical errors at reduced sampling cost.
